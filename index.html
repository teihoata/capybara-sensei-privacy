Capybara Sensei — Privacy Policy

An offline learning game for kids. No data leaves the device. Ever.

Last updated: 11/10/2026 · Applies to Capybara Sensei (Android & iOS), package nz.capybarasensei.app.

The short version: Capybara Sensei does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information — about your child or anyone else — because it has no way to. It runs entirely on the device, has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and (on Android) is built without the INTERNET permission, so it cannot send data anywhere even if a future version tried to.

If you only came here to tick the "privacy policy" box for a device's Family/parental-control settings: there is nothing in this app for those settings to protect your child from.

Who this app is for

Capybara Sensei is designed for young children (roughly ages 5–9) and their families, built around NZ primary-school curriculum content, te reo Māori and introductory Japanese. It is built to meet the spirit of Google Play's Families Policy and comparable "kids' app" guidance: no ads, no purchases, no social features, no external links a child could stumble through, and no data collection of any kind.

Information we collect

None. Specifically:

  • No account, no sign-in, no email address, no name is ever requested — a "player" inside the app is just a local nickname your family types in, stored only on the device.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK of any kind is included. We cannot see how the app is used, how often, by whom, or whether it crashed.
  • No advertising SDK is included. There are no ads, and therefore no ad identifiers, no ad targeting, and no third-party ad networks to share data with.
  • No in-app purchases, and therefore no payment or billing information is ever handled by the app.
  • No network requests at all. On Android the app is built without the INTERNET permission in its manifest, so the operating system itself blocks any attempt to reach the network — this isn't just a policy, it's enforced by the platform. On iOS the app makes no network calls and requests no network-related entitlements.

What is stored, and where

Everything the app remembers — stars earned, stickers unlocked, which words or number facts your child has practised and how well, the island's decorations, settings like music volume or the accessibility options, and which year-level pack is active — is written to the device's own local app storage (the same private, sandboxed storage every app uses for its own files). It:

  • never leaves the device automatically;
  • is not synced to any cloud service, account or server, because the app has none;
  • is only ever shared if you choose to, using the in-app Export button (Settings → Backup), which writes a plain JSON file your device's own share sheet then lets you save or send wherever you choose — the app itself never transmits it;
  • is removed if the app is uninstalled, like any other app's local data.

Microphone, camera, location and other sensitive permissions

Capybara Sensei does not request or use the camera, microphone, contacts, location, or any other sensitive device permission. Spoken instructions are produced by the device (Android/iOS text-to-speech, or a short pre-recorded voice clip bundled inside the app) — nothing is ever recorded or listened to.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either. There is nothing for a parent to request be corrected or deleted beyond simply deleting the app (which removes all of its local data from the device), and nothing is ever shared with or sold to a third party, because nothing is ever sent anywhere in the first place.

Third-party services

This app uses no third-party SDKs, libraries-as-a-service, or analytics/advertising/crash-reporting platforms of any kind. All art is original inline vector graphics or procedural 3D geometry, and all sound is synthesised in code — there are no embedded third-party asset libraries or content-delivery networks either.

Changes to this policy

If this app's data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly. Given the app is fully offline by design, we don't expect that to happen.
